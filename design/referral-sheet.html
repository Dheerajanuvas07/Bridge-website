Referring a client to Bridge

A screened, non-medical Bridge Captain takes an older adult to a non-emergency medical appointment, waits through the visit, brings them home and texts updates to authorized family.

Who Bridge can help

  • Older adults in Lincoln and Omaha with a non-emergency appointment
  • Who can walk with light assistance
  • Who don’t need clinical care during the visit

The older adult decides. We talk with them before the first visit. If they decline, we don’t go.

A Captain will

  • Pick your client up at home
  • Walk in with them and help at check-in
  • Wait through the whole appointment
  • Bring them home and see them settled
  • Text updates to authorized family

A Captain won’t

  • Give medical advice or make clinical decisions
  • Handle or give medication
  • Lift your client or help with transfers
  • Speak for your client

In an emergency, the Captain calls 911, then the family.

Privacy

We share appointment status only with family the older adult has authorized. Updates cover logistics. They aren’t medical records.

Cost

Families pay $149 per standard visit (up to 2 hours, door to door) or $199 for longer or out-of-town visits, directly to Bridge. We don’t bill insurance or Medicare. If the first visit doesn’t go the way we said it would, the family doesn’t pay.

How to refer

Give the family our number, or with their permission, call or text us yourself. We respond within 24 hours and talk with the family and the older adult before anything is booked.

Bridge is a non-medical service. It is not a ride service, home health care or emergency transport. In an emergency, call 911.
