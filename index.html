Standard options leave too many gaps. Someone has to fill them.

The Curb Drop

Rideshares drop them at the door and leave. If the clinic is large or they're confused, they're on their own.

The Check-in Gap

Getting to the building is only half the job. Who makes sure they reach the desk and actually get registered?

The Silent Return

You're in a meeting wondering: Did they get in? Did the appointment happen? Did they make it home?

You shouldn't have to take a half-day off work to ensure a 20-minute appointment actually gets completed.

The Process

Five steps to certainty.

Every Bridge appointment follows the same reliable, end-to-end process.

1

Confirm

We verify appointment details with you and your parent 24 hours ahead.

2

Assist

Your Bridge companion picks them up and walks them directly inside the clinic.

3

Support

We help with check-in and stay through the entire visit.

4

Return

We bring them home safely and make sure they're settled before leaving.

5

Update

You get a short written update the same day.

Trust & Safety

Your parent stays in charge

Before any visit, we talk with your parent. If they say no, we don't go.

Bridge Completion Report

After every visit: checked in, appointment completed, returned home safely. A logistics update — not a medical record.

What Bridge does not do: Bridge is non-medical support only. We do not provide medical advice, administer medication, assist with physical lifting, or make clinical decisions. In an emergency, companions call 911 and notify you immediately.

Pricing

Pilot pricing: we'll talk it through on a call.

Bridge is a small local pilot. Nothing is booked, and nothing is charged, until we've talked with you and your parent.
