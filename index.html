FAQ

Questions families ask before they book.

What's included?

Everything from home arrival to safe return + the 2-hour family update, up to 2.5 hours.

What if the appointment runs long?

Your Captain stays. Extended time is $35/30 min, confirmed by text first.

Do you provide the ride?

You arrange the vehicle (family car, rideshare, or their usual transport). We coordinate timing and ride along — that's what keeps the price flat and the service focused.

Is this medical care?

No. Non-medical companionship only. We document what happened; we don't interpret it or give medical advice.

Can my parent have the same Captain?

Yes — continuity is the default, and it's free.

What does the family update include?

Who attended, what the clinician said, what happens next, and a photo of the after-visit summary — within 2 hours, by text or email.

What is your cancellation policy?

Full refund 48+ hours ahead; 50% fee inside 48 hours.
