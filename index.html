The Process

Five steps to certainty.

Every Bridge appointment follows the same reliable, end-to-end process.

1

Confirm

We verify appointment details with you and your parent 24 hours ahead.

2

Assist

Your Bridge companion picks them up and walks them directly inside the clinic.

3

Support

We help with check-in and stay through the entire visit.

4

Return

We bring them home safely and make sure they're settled before leaving.

5

Update

You get text updates as the visit happens (on the way, checked in, done, home safe) and a short written summary the same day.

Non-medical support

What a companion does and doesn't do

A companion does

  • Picks your parent up at home
  • Walks inside with them
  • Helps with check-in
  • Stays through the visit
  • Brings them home safely
  • Sends family updates

A companion doesn't

  • Give medical advice
  • Handle or give medications
  • Lift or transfer your parent
  • Make clinical decisions
  • Speak for your parent

Emergencies: the companion calls 911, then calls the family.
