06 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Have something else in mind?

Talk to Bridge ↗
Who accompanies my parent?

A background-checked, local Bridge Captain provides non-medical appointment support. We aim for familiar Captain continuity when scheduling allows; Reserved Care families receive preferred matching.

What happens when they arrive at the clinic?

Your Captain walks with your parent into the clinic, helps them reach the right office and check in, and waits during the appointment.

Can Bridge stay during the appointment?

Yes. Your Captain stays through the visit. If your parent needs someone in the examination room, discuss that request when booking so arrangements can be confirmed. Bridge does not provide medical advice or make clinical decisions.

How do I know the appointment was completed?

After the visit, the authorized family contact receives a Bridge Completion Report confirming check-in, appointment completion, and return home. It is a logistics update, not a medical record.

How does transportation work?

Tell us what your parent needs when booking. Options include Captain-provided transportation, travel arranged separately by your family, or assistance at the clinic only. We confirm the arrangements before the appointment.

What if the appointment runs long?

The $89 fee includes up to two hours of appointment support. Your Captain does not leave mid-appointment. For specialist or procedure visits that may take longer, we discuss timing and confirm the details before the appointment.

What does Bridge not provide?

Bridge does not provide medical advice, clinical interpretation, treatment decisions, medication or prescription handling, physical lifting or transfers, or emergency medical transport. In an emergency, Captains call 911.

Can I book recurring appointments?

Yes. Reserved Care offers three appointments for $239, with no expiration and flexible scheduling. The monthly option includes two appointments for $159, priority scheduling, and preferred Captain continuity. You can pause or cancel the monthly plan anytime.
