Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. Information we collect

We collect the information needed to coordinate and deliver Bridge services, including booking form details, appointment logistics, contact information, analytics, and optional notes about mobility or access support.

  • Booking form data such as family and senior details, clinic information, and appointment timing.
  • Contact information such as names, phone numbers, and email addresses.
  • Analytics and cookies used for website performance and basic conversion measurement.
  • Booking and visit notes, including any health-related information shared to help us provide safe support.

2. How we use it

We use personal information to confirm visits, coordinate Captain assignments, communicate with the family, send family updates, improve service quality, and resolve safety or operational issues.

3. Health-related notes

Bridge is a non-medical service. We limit the health-related information we collect to what is necessary to coordinate safe support, such as mobility needs, access instructions, or appointment logistics. We do not share this information beyond the named family contacts and the assigned Captain as needed to complete the visit.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We may share information with a vetted Bridge Captain for service coordination and with the named family contacts who are authorized to receive the appointment update. We may also share information with trusted service providers, such as booking or communication tools, when necessary to operate the website or provide the service.

We do not sell personal information. We may share information if required by law, to protect safety, or in an emergency.

5. Analytics and pixels

We may use privacy-friendly analytics tools to understand traffic, ad performance, and conversion events. We do not use invasive tracking and we keep the analytics setup limited to what is needed to improve the service.

6. Data retention

We keep booking data only as long as needed to provide the service, fulfill our business obligations, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal standards. We delete or securely dispose of information when it is no longer needed.

7. Deletion requests

If you want to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, contact us at hello@bridgelincoln.com. Include the name and email address used for your booking request so we can locate the relevant record.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no digital system is completely risk-free, and we encourage you to reach out if you suspect any data security issue.

9. Cookies

We use a simple cookie banner to remember your privacy choice and to help us monitor website usage. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact hello@bridgelincoln.com or call (402) 555-0140.
