Terms of Service

Effective date: October 2, 2026

1. Service overview

Bridge provides a trusted companion for a parent or older adult attending a medical appointment. This is a non-medical service focused on companionship, arrival support, check-in assistance, the appointment visit, safe return home, and family communication.

2. Price and timing

Bridge charges $139 per visit for up to 2.5 hours. If an appointment runs long, extended time is billed at $35 per 30 minutes and requires confirmation by text before it applies.

3. Non-medical scope

Bridge provides non-medical companionship only. Bridge Captains do not provide medical advice, interpret medical information, handle medications, or perform clinical tasks. They are not a medical provider and do not substitute for a clinician.

In an emergency, a Captain will call 911 and notify the family immediately.

4. Cancellation policy

Full refund 48+ hours ahead; 50% fee inside 48 hours.

5. Consent and authority

By submitting a booking request, you confirm that you are authorized to arrange the service and that the person being supported has consented to a Bridge companion attending the appointment.

6. Customer responsibilities

You are responsible for arranging transportation, confirming appointment details, sharing safety or mobility notes, and communicating any changes as soon as possible. You are also responsible for understanding the service is a non-medical support model.

7. Family updates

Bridge provides a written family update within 2 hours after the appointment. The update may include who attended, what the clinician said, what happens next, and a photo of the after-visit summary when available.

8. Privacy

Bridge uses personal information only for the purpose of coordinating appointment support, communication, and service improvement. Please review our Privacy Policy for details about data collection, retention, deletion requests, and analytics.

9. Limitation of liability

Bridge is not responsible for medical outcomes, clinician decisions, missed diagnoses, or service delays caused by third parties, clinic timing, weather, or other external factors. To the extent permitted by law, Bridge limits liability to the fees paid for the specific service.

10. Contact

If you have questions, contact hello@bridgelincoln.com or call (402) 555-0140.
