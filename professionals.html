For care professionals

A reliable option when your client has no one to take them to an appointment

For social workers, clinic care coordinators, discharge planners, Area Agency on Aging staff and geriatric care managers. Bridge sends a screened, non-clinical companion to take an older adult to a non-emergency medical appointment, stay with them, and bring them home. Lincoln and Omaha, NE.

Who it's for

Who Bridge can help

  • Older adults in Lincoln and Omaha with a non-emergency medical appointment
  • Who can walk with light assistance
  • Who don't need clinical care during the visit

The older adult decides. We talk with them before the first visit. If they decline, we don't go.

Non-medical support

What a companion does and doesn't do

A companion does

  • Picks your client up at home
  • Walks inside with them
  • Helps with check-in
  • Stays through the visit
  • Brings them home safely
  • Sends updates to authorized family

A companion doesn't

  • Give medical advice
  • Handle or give medications
  • Lift or transfer your client
  • Make clinical decisions
  • Speak for your client

Emergencies: the companion calls 911, then calls the family.

Pricing

Families pay $149 per standard visit ($199 extended or rural), directly to Bridge. No insurance billing. First-visit guarantee: if the first visit doesn't go as promised, the family doesn't pay.
